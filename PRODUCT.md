# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

One user: Stephen, the builder and sole operator. He is mid-call when he uses Darin — attention split between a live human conversation and a glanceable second screen. He is an expert in his own tool; nothing needs onboarding, but everything must be legible in a half-second glance while someone is talking to him.

## Product Purpose

Darin is a real-time meeting copilot. During a call it captures both sides (mic + speaker loopback), streams transcription, and surfaces short AI "cards" — proactively when the watcher spots something, and reactively via five action buttons and a freeform Ask box. Post-meeting analysis (summaries, action items, decisions) exists but is secondary; the live in-meeting card feed is the product. Success is a mid-call save: Stephen glances at a card, uses the suggested line, and the conversation goes better than it would have without it.

## Positioning

It hears both sides of a live call and surfaces the right thing to say while it still matters. Every screen reinforces in-the-moment usefulness — not record-keeping, not analytics.

## Brand Personality

Alert, tactical, fast. Darin feels like a sharp operator at your elbow during a high-stakes call: quick reads, clear urgency signals, no ceremony. Energy matches the stakes of a live conversation — but the call always has the floor; Darin's intensity lives in its readiness, not in visual noise.

## Anti-references

- **Chatbot UI** — no chat-bubble aesthetics, no assistant avatar personality, no conversational back-and-forth framing. Darin is an instrument, not an interlocutor.
- **SaaS dashboard** — no KPI-widget grids, hero metrics, or analytics-tool chrome. The live feed is a stream of moments, not a report.

## Design Principles

1. **Glance-legible or gone.** Every card must deliver its payload in under three seconds of split attention. If an element needs study, it fails — cut it or restructure it.
2. **Say-this first.** The actionable line is the payload. Hierarchy puts what to say above why; supporting context is subordinate, collapsible, or absent.
3. **The call has the floor.** Darin never competes with the human conversation for attention. Urgency is earned per-card, not ambient; motion conveys arrival and state, never decoration.
4. **Speed reads as competence.** Instant feedback on every action, skeletons over spinners, no waiting choreography. A tactical tool that hesitates loses trust.
5. **Built for its one expert.** Density, shortcuts, and configurability over hand-holding. Labels can be terse; the operator wrote them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text) as the working standard, visible focus states on all interactive elements, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation. Glance-legibility under split attention is treated as an accessibility requirement in its own right.
